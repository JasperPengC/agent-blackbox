# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in this project should be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
appearance, body size, race, age, religion or technical preference.

## Expected behaviour

- Discuss technical decisions on their merits; never judge people.
- It is fine to say "I don't know", and fine for others to say it too.
- Provide reproducible evidence (logs, commands, versions) instead of "it does not
  work for me".
- Accept that maintainers may reject features to keep scope under control. The
  project's non-goals are deliberate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, belittling, political or religious arguments.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information, including session-log screenshots.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for behaviour they consider inappropriate. Report incidents
through GitHub's private security advisory or by contacting a maintainer directly.
